## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet authority, mechanical mastery, and timeless prestige of elite Swiss haute horlogerie. It caters to connoisseurs, collectors, and patrons of micro-engineering who appreciate technical perfection and bespoke luxury. 

The aesthetic style merges modern luxury minimalism with tactile craftsmanship:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian recesses accented with liquid-like champagne gold and soft platinum light, creating the intimate, high-focus environment of an exclusive private atelier or vault.
- **Precision Detailing:** Fine, microscopic hair-thin dividers (0.5px to 1px) mimicking beveled watch indices, metallic radial brushed textures, and architectural structural grids.
- **Subtle Materiality:** Frosted dark crystalline surfaces with edge-highlighted golden gradients, conveying physical substance without ornamental clutter.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from blackened titanium, deep obsidian dials, and warm hand-finished champagne gold alloy.

### Primary Roles & Accents
- **Primary Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#E5C378`):** Reserved for paramount interactions, high-value metrics, certification stamps, and fine border illumination. Never applied over broad background fields.
- **Secondary Champagne (`#C5A059`):** Used for secondary interactions, micro-labels, and subtle gradient stops.
- **Tertiary Slate (`#232731`):** Applied to structural dividers, inactive track states, and card outlines.

### Dark Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Background Base (`#090A0C`):** The foundational infinite-depth backdrop.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#111318`):** Canvas for elevated panels, drawers, and modular displays.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#181B22`):** Interactive card fills, input housings, and popovers.

### Typography & Iconography
- **Platinum Bright (`#F5F5F7`):** Primary editorial copy, headlines, and primary figures.
- **Muted Steel (`#A1A1AA`):** Body paragraphs, movement descriptions, and specifications.
- **Subtle Slate (`#71717A`):** Footnotes, technical caliber codes, and inactive indicators.

## Typography

The type system balances classical horological prestige with surgical, technical legibility:

- **Headlines & Narrative (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys bespoke heritage and human craftsmanship. It should be typeset with relaxed breathing room and restrained weights (predominantly Regular 400 and Medium 500) to maintain an understated, aristocratic poise.
- **UI, Metrics & Technical Specs (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and functional. All uppercase labels, movement references (e.g., `CALIBRE 8001`), and horological specs use wide letter spacing (`0.12em` to `0.15em`) to evoke the millimeter markings of a tachymeter or luxury bezel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a structured 12-column grid, prioritizing wide margins, intentional negative space, and architectural symmetry.

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Max-width centered container of `1360px` with `2rem` gutters. Horological spec grids and complication breakdowns default to balanced 2, 3, or 4-column balanced layouts. Generous vertical spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) frames timepieces like artifacts in a curated gallery.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Side-by-side watch configurations reflow to staggered vertical pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins. Interactive components stack strictly vertically with `space-xl` separation, ensuring touch targets exceed 48px while maintaining an uncluttered aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent material tiers and delicate linear illumination rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layers:** 
  - `Base`: `#090A0C` (Dial depth).
  - `Mid-Level`: `#111318` with 80% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` for floating navigation bars and modal overlays.
  - `Top-Level`: `#181B22` for interactive cards and micro-spec sheets.
- **Gold-Glint Highlights:** Cards and prominent panels employ a fine gradient border (`1px solid transparent`, masked via `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(212,175,55,0.3) 0%, rgba(35,39,49,0.4) 50%, rgba(212,175,55,0.05) 100%)`). This mimics light catching the hand-polished bevel of a platinum or gold watch case.
- **Ambient Glow:** Critical floating action surfaces project a soft, expansive, low-opacity gold aura: `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry. 

- Sharp mechanical angles are tempered with subtle corner radii (`0.25rem` / `4px` base) to reflect sapphire crystals and beveled watch lugs.
- Cards, dialogue panes, and media enclosures use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Circular elements are strictly reserved for horological complications, watch dials, crown selectors, and precision certification emblems. Pill shapes are eschewed in favor of refined, razor-precise rectangular boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Atelier CTA):** Finished in champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) with dark obsidian typography (`#090A0C`, weight 600). Interactive hover transitions into lighter gold (`#E5C378`) paired with a faint radial outer glow.
- **Secondary (Technical / Inquire):** Dark background (`#111318`) enclosed by a `1px` subtle champagne gold border (`rgba(212,175,55,0.3)`), shifting to full opacity gold border on hover with platinum text (`#F5F5F7`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Unfilled, accompanied by a subtle tracking arrow or crosshair icon with text in `#A1A1AA`, transitioning to `#D4AF37`.

### Cards & Spec Sheets
- **Frosted Specimen Card:** Layered on `#181B22` with a `1px` gradient border fading from `#D4AF37` to `#232731`. Interior contains watch caliber specs, crystal details, and power reserve metrics separated by fine `0.5px` dividers (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`).

### Micro-Badges & Swiss-Certified Emblems
- **Swiss Precision Badge:** Minimal, sharp rectangular badge (`roundedness: 0.25rem`) with `0.15em` letter-spacing, `#D4AF37` border, `#111318` background, and monochromatic gold typography (e.g., `CHRONOMÈTRE CERTIFIÉ`, `LIMITED TO 25 PIECES`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Inputs:** Dark obsidian wells (`#111318`) with hair-thin borders (`#232731`). On focus, borders illuminate softly in `#D4AF37` without standard browser rings. Placeholder text rests at `#71717A`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist mechanical indicators. Radio buttons mimic index dots with a central champagne gold core when selected; checkboxes use an ultra-thin angled checkmark with gold perimeter accents.

### Complication Counters & Metrics
- Numerical values (jewels, frequencies in vph, power reserves) render in sharp, clean typography with high-contrast `#F5F5F7` numerals and subdued `#71717A` uppercase unit markers beneath.
